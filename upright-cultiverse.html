Grown indoors, harvested at peak freshness

Every head of lettuce and every bunch of basil is grown in a controlled, clean indoor environment — zero soil contaminants, zero chemical pesticides. No open fields, no unpredictable weather, no compromise on what ends up on the plate.

Because we harvest right here in the city, produce reaches your kitchen within hours, not days — maximizing shelf life and cutting the spoilage that eats into your margins.
